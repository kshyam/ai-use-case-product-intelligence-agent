## Overview

**Product Intelligence Agent** is a reference AI workflow for Technology & SaaS organizations. It addresses a practical product challenge:

> Product feedback is fragmented across tickets, reviews, analytics, sales calls and feature requests.

The workflow is triggered when **New feedback or scheduled intelligence review**. It coordinates Feedback clustering, trend and impact agents and keeps people responsible for decisions that require judgment, exception handling, or a sensitive business action.

## Why This AI Workflow Matters

Organizations often have the data and systems needed to solve this problem, but the information is spread across teams and operational tools. An AI agent workflow can help bring the right context together, explain the next step, and carry out approved actions without hiding the decision trail.

This is an architecture pattern, not a promise of automatic results. Production implementation depends on data quality, system access, workflow evaluation, security controls, and a clear human review design.

## How It Works

1. **Collect approved feedback sources**
2. **Cluster recurring problems**
3. **Identify emerging trends**
4. **Estimate opportunity impact**
5. **Draft a product requirement for review**

## Human Oversight and Security Controls

**Human checkpoint:** Validate themes, prioritize opportunities and approve product requirements.

Recommended controls for this AI agent use case:

- Source permissions
- Evidence links
- Human prioritization
- Backlog approval

These controls define what the AI system can see, recommend, or do, and when a person must review the outcome. High-impact actions should fail closed when identity, policy, or approval information is missing.
